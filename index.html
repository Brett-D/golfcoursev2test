Central Oregon Coast

Nine holes of coastal golf, open year round.

Playing since 1931 just north of Newport, Agate Beach Golf Course pairs gently rolling fairways and ocean air with a welcoming clubhouse, full pro shop and a café that opens with the sunrise.

A classic layout that plays well for everyone

Frank Stenzel's 1931 routing threads two par 3s, five par 4s and two par 5s through wooded terrain a short walk from the Pacific. It is short enough to walk comfortably and interesting enough to keep low handicaps honest.

  • Play nine or loop twice for a full eighteen
  • Iron-only driving range and practice green
  • Right- and left-handed rental clubs, carts and pull carts
  • Mild coastal weather keeps us open all twelve months
