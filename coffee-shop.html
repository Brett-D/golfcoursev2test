Coffee Shop

The Café at Agate Beach

Espresso poured at sunrise, breakfast off the griddle and lunch to take on the course — no tee time required.

Coffee first, golf second

Our café sits just inside the clubhouse doors, looking out over the first tee. Golfers grab a coffee before the round and a sandwich after; neighbours and beach walkers stop in simply because the espresso is good and the seat by the window is warm.

  • Open daily, 7:00 AM – 2:00 PM
  • Breakfast served until 11:00 AM, lunch from 11:00 AM
  • Grab-and-go snacks and cold drinks all day
  • Call ahead on (541) 265-7331 and we will have it ready at the turn
