Come enjoy a delicious burger before or after your game!

Breakfast and lunch served at the clubhouse — smash burgers, house-roasted turkey, griddled hot cakes, soup of the moment and more. You don’t need a tee time to stop in.

Alcoholic beverages consumed on the course must be purchased through our restaurant according to Oregon law.
