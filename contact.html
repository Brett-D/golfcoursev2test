Getting here

From Highway 101, look for the clubhouse on the east side of the road at the north end of Newport, just past the Agate Beach turnoff. Parking is free and the first tee is a short walk from the lot.

While you are on the coast

Agate Beach State Recreation Site, Yaquina Head Lighthouse and Nye Beach are all within a ten-minute drive — an easy morning round followed by an afternoon at the ocean.

Tournaments

Shotgun starts, scrambles and league nights for groups of twelve or more.

Junior golf

Summer clinics and discounted junior rates to grow the next generation of players.

Gift cards

Available in the pro shop and good for golf, gear and café orders alike.
