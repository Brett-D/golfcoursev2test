Passes & punch cards

Regulars save with a punch card, and annual passes are available for players who call Agate Beach home.

  • 10-round punch card — one round free
  • Monthly unlimited play for local residents
  • Annual pass with pro shop discount
  • Group and tournament pricing for 12 or more

Ask at the counter or send us a note and we will walk you through the options.
