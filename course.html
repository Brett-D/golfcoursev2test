Course & Practice

The Course

Nine holes, par 36, laid out by Frank Stenzel in 1931 and shaped by nearly a century of coastal weather.

How it plays

The routing rolls gently across wooded ground a half mile from the surf. Two par 3s, five par 4s and two par 5s give you a mix of every club in the bag without a punishing walk. Play it once for a quick nine, or use the alternate women's tees on the second loop for a different look at eighteen.

  • Men: par 36, 2,905 yards
  • Women: par 38, 2,669 yards
  • Walking friendly; carts and pull carts available
  • Open all year — coastal winters stay playable

Practice & instruction

Driving range

An iron-only range for grooving your mid and short game. Buckets are sold at the pro shop counter.

Practice green

Roll putts and dial in chip shots on the same coastal poa you will face on the course.

Lessons

Individual and group instruction with our PGA professional. Call the shop to book a time that suits you.

Course etiquette

A few house rules that keep play moving and the course in shape for everyone behind you.

  • Repair divots and ball marks
  • Keep pace with the group ahead, not the group behind
  • Carts stay on paths near tees and greens
  • Soft spikes only, please
